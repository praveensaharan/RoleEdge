# Voice recording script (for your cloned voice)

These recordings train Meta's open voice model (MMS-TTS) to speak in your voice. Training runs on Modal.

## How to record

- **Where:** a quiet room, phone about 20 cm from your mouth, no music or fan.
- **Format:** one file per line, named `001.wav`, `002.wav` and so on. Any recorder app is fine; I convert the files
  to 16 kHz mono.
- **Voice:** read naturally, as if talking to your mother on the phone. Not too fast; a short pause at each full stop.
- **Mistakes:** if you stumble, just record that line again.
- **Where the files go:** `private/voice/` on this machine. That folder is never committed and stays out of the
  public repository.
- **Time:** about 10–15 minutes in total. 100 lines is enough; all 120 is better.
